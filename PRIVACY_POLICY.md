## 1. Overview (English)

**Mafia App (`مافيا`)** is an offline pass-and-play tabletop utility designed to distribute secret game roles during in-person social deduction game sessions. We respect your privacy and have engineered the application to operate **100% offline** with **zero data collection**.

### 1.1 No Personal Data Collection
- The application **does not** collect, transmit, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, or usage analytics.
- The application **does not** require user registration, accounts, or sign-in of any kind.

### 1.2 100% Offline Operation & Zero Third-Party Trackers
- The application functions entirely offline on your device and makes no network requests to external servers.
- The application contains **no advertisements**, **no in-app purchases**, and **no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs**.

### 1.3 Local Device Storage (`SharedPreferences`)
- To improve user convenience, the application stores only your last selected game configuration (total player count between 6 and 18, and selected optional game roles) locally on your device using standard Android/iOS local preferences (`SharedPreferences`).
- This configuration data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the application.

### 1.4 Children's Privacy
- Because the application collects no personal data whatsoever from any user, it complies with applicable children's and general privacy regulations (including GDPR and COPPA).

### 1.5 Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our official GitHub repository:  
https://github.com/Someooo/Mafia-app/issues
